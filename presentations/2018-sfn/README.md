# Apolipoprotein ɛ4 Carrier Status Impacts Affective and Cognitive Empathy

**Poster Presentation** ([View Poster](./2018-chow-sfn.png)) | Society for Neuroscience (SfN) Annual Meeting | 2018 | San Diego, CA, USA

**Tools:** `R` `nlme`

**Core Skills:** `Longitudinal Trajectory Modeling` `Linear Mixed-Effects Models` `Multivariate Linear Regression` `Cohort Stratification` `Multimodal Data Integration`

## Executive Summary

* **Problem:** Alzheimer's disease (AD) is a neurodegenerative disorder that involves progressive cognitive decline alongside socioemotional alterations, including changes in empathy. The Apolipoprotein ɛ4 (APOE\*E4) allele is the strongest genetic risk factor for developing sporadic AD, but whether APOE\*E4 relates to individual differences in affective and cognitive empathy trajectories in healthy older adults prior to cognitive decline is unknown.
* **Approach:** Evaluated a large, longitudinal cohort of cognitively healthy older adults (*n* = 359) over follow-up intervals ranging up to 12.4 years. Conducted multivariate linear regressions to measure baseline differences in informant-rated affective and cognitive empathy as well as longitudinal linear mixed-effects models to test the interaction between APOE\*E4 status and time on empathy, while tracking neuropsychological performance across episodic memory and executive function domains.
* **Takeaway:** Demonstrated that genetic risk for AD impacts socioemotional profiles before clinical onset: **APOE\*E4 carriers** exhibited significantly lower baseline affective and cognitive empathy relative to **non-carriers**, yet displayed a steeper longitudinal increase in cognitive empathy across time. Crucially, these socioemotional shifts emerged in the complete absence of concurrent cognitive or neuropsychological decline, identifying potential early behavioral phenotypes of AD risk.

## Technical Methodologies
*As the lead researcher and first author, I designed and conducted the end-to-end analytical, statistical, and visualization workflow:*
* **Longitudinal Trajectory and Statistical Modeling:** Designed and implemented a comparative analysis pipeline to evaluate socioemotional divergence based on genetic AD risk. Developed multivariate linear regressions and linear mixed-effects models in **R** to evaluate baseline group differences and longitudinal trajectories on multidimensional measures of empathy and cognition.
* **Data Wrangling and Multimodal Integration:** Investigated the core research question and operationalized the analytical design. Curated and integrated longitudinal data across 359 older adults that included harmonizing APOE\*E4 genotyping, annual neuropsychological test batteries, and informant-based empathy evaluations.
* **Data Visualization:** Authored all presentation materials, creating longitudinal trajectory plots and baseline comparative bar charts in **R** to demonstrate the genetic impacts of APOE\*E4 on socioemotional and cognitive profiles.
